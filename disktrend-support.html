Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a folder or drive?
Choose File > Open… (or the "Choose Folder or Volume…" button) and select the folder or volume you'd like DiskTrend to scan. DiskTrend will show its contents sized largest to smallest.
Why does DiskTrend ask for permission to access a folder?
DiskTrend runs inside Apple's App Sandbox, so it can only see folders you've explicitly granted it access to. Once you grant access, DiskTrend remembers it (via a security-scoped bookmark) so you won't be asked again for that same location.
What is the size-history / trend feature?
DiskTrend can track how a folder's size changes over time, showing 1-day, 1-week, and 1-month trend badges as you rescan. This history is stored only on your Mac. You can turn it off, adjust how deep it tracks, or delete the history database entirely from Settings.
How does the Duplicate Finder work?
Duplicate Finder scans the folder you have open for files with identical content and lets you review and move the extras to the Trash, freeing up space without touching the copy you keep.
What does the Cache Cleaner do?
Cache Cleaner finds common developer and system caches — like Xcode DerivedData — that are safe to clear, and lets you move them to the Trash or empty the Trash directly. Emptying the Trash is permanent, so review what's selected first.
If I delete something, can I get it back?
Yes — by default, deleting a file or folder in DiskTrend moves it to the macOS Trash, just like Finder, so it's recoverable until you empty the Trash.
Does DiskTrend send any of my data anywhere?
No. DiskTrend has no account, no server, and makes no network requests. Everything, including size-history data, stays on your Mac. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
The app isn't working as expected — what should I do?
Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue persists, email us at tucmetcalf@gmail.com with a description of the problem and we'll look into it right away.
