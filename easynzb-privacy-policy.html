EasyNZB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

EasyNZB is a Usenet (NNTP) download client for macOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information EasyNZB collects when you use the app — in short, none beyond what's needed to connect to the Usenet server(s) you configure.

1. Information We Collect

EasyNZB does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal information. There is no EasyNZB account, no sign-in, and no EasyNZB-operated server that the app communicates with. EasyNZB does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. The only network connections EasyNZB makes are the Usenet (NNTP) server connections you configure in Preferences, used solely to download the content you request from your own Usenet provider.

2. Usenet Server Credentials

Server details you enter — display name, host, port, and connection settings — are stored locally in EasyNZB's preferences on your Mac. Your username and password are stored in the macOS Keychain, and are only ever transmitted directly to the Usenet server you configured, over the connection (SSL by default) you configured for it. EasyNZB has no server of its own that ever sees these credentials.

3. Folder Access

EasyNZB runs inside Apple's App Sandbox. It can only see the download folder you explicitly choose via the "Choose…" picker in Preferences. To avoid re-prompting you on every launch, macOS lets EasyNZB keep access to that folder via a security-scoped bookmark — this is a standard sandboxing mechanism, and it never extends EasyNZB's access beyond what you've explicitly selected.

4. Download Queue & History

EasyNZB keeps a local record of your download queue and completed jobs — filenames and status — so you can review them in the app. This is stored entirely on your Mac and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere.

5. Notifications

EasyNZB can show local notifications (e.g. "Download Complete", "Download Failed") via the macOS Notification Center. These are generated entirely on your Mac — no third-party push or notification service is involved.

6. Third-Party Tools

If you enable automatic PAR2 repair or archive extraction in Preferences, EasyNZB runs external command-line tools (par2 and unar) that you install yourself, typically via Homebrew. These run locally on your Mac against the files you've downloaded — no data leaves your Mac as part of this step.

7. Children's Privacy

EasyNZB has no age restriction. Because EasyNZB collects no personal information from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
