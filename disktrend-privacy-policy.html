DiskTrend

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DiskTrend is a disk usage and cleanup utility for macOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information DiskTrend collects when you use the app — in short, none.

1. Information We Collect

DiskTrend does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that DiskTrend communicates with. DiskTrend does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests of any kind.

2. Folder and File Access

DiskTrend runs inside Apple's App Sandbox. It can only see the folders and volumes you explicitly choose through the standard macOS "Choose Folder" picker. To avoid re-prompting you on every launch, macOS lets DiskTrend keep access to a folder you've already granted via a security-scoped bookmark — this is a standard sandboxing mechanism, and it never extends DiskTrend's access beyond what you've explicitly selected.

3. Local Size-History Data

DiskTrend can optionally keep a history of folder sizes over time, so it can show you how a folder has grown or shrunk over the past day, week, or month. This history is stored entirely on your Mac, in a local database in your user Library folder. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere. You can turn history tracking off, or permanently delete the history database, at any time from DiskTrend's Settings.

4. Deleting Files

When you delete items with DiskTrend — whether individually, via the Duplicate Finder, or via the Cache Cleaner — they are moved to the macOS Trash by default, the same as deleting a file in Finder, so they remain recoverable until the Trash is emptied. DiskTrend does not transmit any information about deleted files anywhere.

5. Children's Privacy

DiskTrend has no age restriction. Because DiskTrend collects no personal information from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
