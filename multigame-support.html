Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?
No. Every game works without signing in. Signing in (email, Apple, Google or Facebook) is optional.
How do I play against someone else?
Open a board game (Chess, Checkers, Othello, Connect Four, Backgammon or Tic-Tac-Toe) and pick a mode on the setup screen: 1 Player vs AI, 2 Players pass & play on one device, or 2 Players multiplayer — nearby over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, or online.
How does online play work?
Choose Online, then New match: give the match a name and send the 6-character code to your opponent, who enters it under Join a match. The match is saved, so either of you can leave and come back later — reopen it from My matches (the people icon). You can have several matches going at once.
How do I switch between 1 and 2 players, or change the AI difficulty?
During a board game, tap the Change mode button (the sliders icon) in the top bar to return to the setup screen.
Where can I see leaderboards and achievements?
Tap the Rankings button in a game, or open More > Game Center. Leaderboards and achievements need you to be signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android).
Why doesn't my progress appear on my other device?
Level progress, solitaire statistics and saved games are stored on each device. Online matches and Game Center scores are shared across devices.
Can I change the app's look?
MultiGame follows the light or dark theme chosen in Settings. In Solitaire, the palette icon on the variant list lets you choose a table background.
How do I delete my account?
Open More > Account and choose Delete account. This removes your account and its data from our server. See our Privacy Policy for details.
The app isn't working as expected — what should I do?
Try closing and reopening the app first. If the problem continues, send feedback from inside the app, or email us at tucmetcalf@gmail.com with a description of the problem. Uploading logs from Settings helps us find the cause.
