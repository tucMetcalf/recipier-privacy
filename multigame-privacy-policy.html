MultiGame

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MultiGame is a collection of arcade, puzzle, board and card games for iPhone, iPad and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information MultiGame collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we need to make the games work.

1. Information We Collect

MultiGame does not show advertising and does not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. Depending on which features you use, it stores the following:

  • An anonymous account. When you first open the app it creates an anonymous account on our server so online features work. It is identified by a random ID only — no name, email or contact details.
  • An optional signed-in account. If you choose to sign in with an email address, Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, we store your email address and display name as provided by that service. Signing in is never required to play.
  • Online matches. When you play a board game online, we store the match name you choose, the two players' display names, every move, and any chat messages sent during the match, so either player can resume it. Only the two players in a match can see it.
  • Match history. After an online match, the result (win, loss or draw) and your opponent's display name are recorded so you can see your head-to-head record.
  • Feedback you send. If you use the in-app feedback form, we store your message, any screenshots you attach, the app version and your device platform.
  • Diagnostic logs, only if you send them. If you tap “Upload logs” in Settings, recent app logs (error messages, app version and a device identifier) are sent to help us fix problems. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to.

2. Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your scores and achievements are submitted to Apple or Google so leaderboards and achievements work, and your public alias is shown to opponents in online matches. That information is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Your level progress, stars, best times, solitaire statistics, saved games and settings are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our server.

4. Nearby (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi) Play

When you play someone nearby, the two devices connect directly to each other. Moves and chat sent this way go only between the two devices and are not stored on our server.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to run the app's features — online matches, match history, sign-in and support — and to fix problems you report. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

6. Where Your Data Is Stored

Online data is stored on a private server operated by us. Access is restricted so that each player can only read their own account, matches and history.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the app, which removes your account and the data linked to it from our server. The player who created an online match can also delete it from My matches, which removes it for both players. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

MultiGame has no age restriction and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Signing in is optional, so children can play without providing any personal details. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
